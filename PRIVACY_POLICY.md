# Privacy Policy for Tabattal (تبتّل)

**Effective Date: August 2026**

Welcome to **Tabattal** (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains my practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the App.

Tabattal was built as a passion project and a *Sadaqa Jariya* (continuous charity). My core principle is absolute privacy, zero tracking, and an uninterrupted, distraction-free Quranic experience.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

**I do not collect, store, or share any personal data.**

The App operates locally on your device. It does not require you to create an account, does not track your behavior, and does not harvest any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

---

### 2. Photos, Videos, and Device Storage (Media Studio & Sharing)

Tabattal includes creative media tools, such as the **Quran Video Studio**, **Verse Card Designer**, and **Mushaf Page Exporter**. 

- **Selecting Background Media:** The App allows you to choose images or videos from your local device gallery to use as backgrounds for video creation. This access is facilitated through the standard Android Photo Picker.
- **Saving Rendered Media:** The App saves exported video reels, verse cards, and Mushaf page snapshots directly to your device's photo gallery / local storage.
- **100% On-Device Processing:** All video encoding and image rendering (powered by the integrated FFmpeg engine) take place **entirely on your local device**. I **do not** upload, transmit, process, or store your photos, videos, or exported media on any external server or third-party service.

---

### 3. Network Access and Downloaded Content

The App requires network/internet access strictly for essential functional features requested by the user:
- **Quranic Audio Recitations:** To stream or download audio files for offline listening.
- **Tafsir & Translations:** To fetch Tafsir or translation databases when requested.
- **In-App Updates:** To check for critical app updates via Google Play services or repository configuration.

None of your recitation listening habits, search queries, bookmarks, or download histories are transmitted to me or any third party.

---

### 4. Background Audio & Foreground Service

To provide continuous, uninterrupted audio recitation while multitasking or when your screen is locked, Tabattal uses the Android `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK` service. This service is used exclusively for playing Quranic audio and providing standard lock-screen and notification playback controls.

---

### 5. Advertising and Analytics

Tabattal is **100% Ad-Free**. I do not use third-party advertising networks, nor do I integrate third-party analytics or tracking SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Facebook Pixel).

---

### 6. Children’s Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal information and contains no advertisements or user-generated public communication channels, it is completely safe for children of all ages. Tabattal complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy standards.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published in the App's official repository. Your continued use of the App following any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
